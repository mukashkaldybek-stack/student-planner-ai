# Student Planner — Project Brainstorm

## 1. Project Idea
Student Planner is a web application that helps students organize academic tasks, manage deadlines, and track their study progress.

## 2. Problem
Students often struggle to manage assignments, remember deadlines, and organize their study schedules.

## 3. Target Users
- University students
- College students
- Students who want to organize their academic work

## 4. Core Features
- Create and manage academic tasks
- Set deadlines for assignments
- Track task completion status
- View upcoming deadlines
- Organize study activities

## 5. Future AI Features
- Suggest a study schedule
- Prioritize tasks based on deadlines
- Provide personalized study recommendations

## 6. Initial Scope
The first version will focus on basic task management, deadlines, and task status tracking.

Advanced AI features may be considered in later versions.
7. Brainstorming Process

Step 1: Identify a Problem

I wanted to explore an application idea that solves a practical problem for students.

Step 2: Explore Possible Ideas

I considered problems related to organizing academic work, managing deadlines, and keeping track of study progress.

Step 3: Select the App Idea

I selected Student Planner because students regularly need to manage assignments and remember deadlines.

Step 4: Define the Target Users

The primary users are university and college students who want a simple way to organize their academic responsibilities.

Step 5: Define the MVP

The first version will focus on creating, viewing, editing, and deleting tasks, setting deadlines, and tracking completion status.

Step 6: Identify Future Improvements

AI-generated study schedules, deadline-based prioritization, and personalized recommendations are potential future features. They are excluded from the initial MVP.

Step 7: Prepare for Specification

The selected idea will be documented in _docs/plan.md. The project requirements and technology choices will be reviewed before implementation begins.
